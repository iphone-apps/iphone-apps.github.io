(No answer typed yet)
📖 SAMPLE ANSWER:

Justice is the foundation of any civilized society. It ensures that every individual is treated fairly and equally under the law. Without justice, there would be chaos, oppression, and discrimination. A just society protects the rights of its citizens, punishes wrongdoers, and provides remedies to those who have been wronged. It promotes peace, stability, and social harmony. Justice also acts as a deterrent against crime and corruption. In Pakistan, the pursuit of justice is a fundamental right guaranteed by the Constitution. However, achieving true justice requires an independent judiciary, transparent legal processes, and a commitment to upholding the rule of law. When justice prevails, society thrives.
