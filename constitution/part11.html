Article 227
Provisions relating to the Holy Quran and Sunnah

227. (1) All existing laws shall be brought in conformity with the Injunctions of Islam as laid down in the Holy Quran and Sunnah, in this Part referred to as the Injunctions of Islam, and no law shall be enacted which is repugnant to such Injunctions.

[Explanation.—In the application of this clause to the personal law of any Muslim sect, the expression "Quran and Sunnah" shall mean the Quran and Sunnah as interpreted by that sect.]

(2) Effect shall be given to the provisions of clause (1) only in the manner provided in this Part.

(3) Nothing in this Part shall affect the personal laws of non-Muslim citizens or their status as citizens.

📖 Explanation:

Article 227 declares that all existing laws shall be brought in conformity with the Injunctions of Islam as laid down in the Holy Quran and Sunnah, and no law repugnant to such Injunctions shall be enacted. It also protects the personal laws of non-Muslim citizens.

Article 228
Composition of Council of Islamic Ideology

228. (1) There shall be constituted a Council of Islamic Ideology, in this Part referred to as the Islamic Council, consisting of such number of members as the President may determine, but not less than eight and not more than twenty.

(2) The members of the Islamic Council shall be appointed by the President from amongst persons having knowledge of the principles and philosophy of Islam as enunciated in the Holy Quran and Sunnah, or understanding of the economic, political, legal or administrative problems of Pakistan.

(3) While appointing members of the Islamic Council, the President shall ensure that—

(a) so far as practicable, not less than one-third of the members are persons having knowledge of the principles and philosophy of Islam as enunciated in the Holy Quran and Sunnah;

(b) not less than two members are persons having knowledge of the economic, political, legal or administrative problems of Pakistan;

(c) not less than one member is a woman; and

(d) not less than one member is a non-Muslim.

(4) The President shall appoint one of the members of the Islamic Council to be the Chairman of the Islamic Council.

📖 Explanation:

Article 228 establishes the Council of Islamic Ideology, consisting of 8 to 20 members appointed by the President. Members must have knowledge of Islam or understanding of Pakistan's economic, political, legal, or administrative problems. At least one member must be a woman and one must be a non-Muslim.

Article 229
Reference by President to Islamic Council, etc.

229. (1) The President or the Governor of a Province may refer to the Islamic Council for advice any question as to whether a proposed law is or is not repugnant to the Injunctions of Islam.

(2) When the Council receives a reference under clause (1), it shall, after taking into consideration the provisions of the Constitution and the law relating to the matter, and after consulting such persons as it may consider necessary, submit its advice to the President or, as the case may be, the Governor within thirty days or such longer period as the President or the Governor may allow.

(3) The advice of the Council shall not be binding on the President or the Governor.

📖 Explanation:

Article 229 allows the President or a Governor to refer any proposed law to the Council of Islamic Ideology for advice on whether it is repugnant to the Injunctions of Islam. The Council must submit its advice within 30 days. The advice is not binding.

Article 230
Functions of the Islamic Council

230. (1) The functions of the Islamic Council shall be—

(a) to make recommendations to [Majlis-e-Shoora (Parliament)] and the Provincial Assemblies as to the ways and means of enabling and encouraging the Muslims of Pakistan to order their lives individually and collectively in all respects in accordance with the principles and concepts of Islam as enunciated in the Holy Quran and Sunnah;

(b) to advise a House, a Provincial Assembly, the President or a Governor on any question referred to the Council under Article 229; and

(c) to make recommendations for the proper organisation of Zakat, Ushr, Auqaf, mosques and other religious institutions and for the promotion of unity and solidarity among the Muslims.

(2) The Council shall, in the performance of its functions under this Article, be guided by the principles and concepts of Islam as enunciated in the Holy Quran and Sunnah.

📖 Explanation:

Article 230 defines the functions of the Council of Islamic Ideology: (1) to recommend ways to enable Muslims to order their lives according to Islam, (2) to advise on questions referred to it, and (3) to recommend proper organization of Zakat, Ushr, Auqaf, and mosques.

Article 231
Rules of procedure

231. The proceedings of the Islamic Council shall be regulated by rules of procedure to be made by the Council with the approval of the President.

📖 Explanation:

Article 231 provides that the proceedings of the Council of Islamic Ideology shall be regulated by rules made by the Council with the approval of the President.
